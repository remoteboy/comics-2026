# Publisher and creator series browsing

Publisher detail pages list series titles, with publisher-specific issue, cover and owned-copy counts. Selecting a series opens its collection page.

Creator detail pages group credited covers under expandable series titles. Expanding a series loads that creator's credited issues; other creators' issues are excluded. The list uses numeric issue ordering, including fractions, and offers Load more issues for longer lists. View credited issues opens a filtered page with ordinary pagination, which also works without JavaScript.

Both pages have a Series title filter with autocomplete. The results narrow as you type, including matching series on later pages; selecting a suggestion is optional. Creator pages also have a Role filter, including unspecified credits, which updates the list immediately. Suggestions are limited to series associated with the current publisher or creator and respect the selected role.

For example, open Eric Powell, type Action Comics in Series title, choose the relevant series suggestion, and expand it. Choose a role if you want only writing or cover-art credits. All linked collection records are included; there is no separate ownership filter.

Typing waits briefly before refreshing the list; role changes refresh immediately. Focus remains in the title field, and older responses cannot replace newer results. Filters reset pagination and remain applied when loading more issues. Clear filters returns to all series for the current record. Publisher/creator renaming and merging remain available above the results.

With JavaScript disabled, Apply filters still submits the filter form normally.

No additional migration is required; this follows creator/publisher management and migration 0010.
